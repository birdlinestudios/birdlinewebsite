FAQ

Questions Before You Start.

What is Birdline?
Birdline combines a premium creative studio with an AI-native production workspace. Our Monthly Partnership includes a dedicated creative team, unlimited editing and design deliverables, and the workspace to connect briefs, content, and performance insights. The workspace is also available independently for brands working with their own teams and creative partners.
What counts as a deliverable?
Any video edit, cutdown, design, or animation your team requests through the workspace: paid social ads, organic clips, statics, landing page graphics, motion pieces, and the iterations on all of them. Productions that need a shoot (video, photography, UGC) are scoped separately, per project.
How fast do deliverables come back?
Most edits come back within the week, and many sooner. Bigger pieces get a timeline up front. Every request has an owner, a due date, and a review status in the workspace, so you always know when it lands.
Why wouldn't I just hire a full-time editor?
Hiring an editor means covering salary, benefits, software, and management for one role. Birdline gives you access to editors, designers, and animators, plus unlimited deliverables, project management, and our workspace through one Monthly Partnership. You get broader creative support without the cost of hiring and managing an entire team.
Do I own the media and the project files?
Yes. You own everything we create under the agreement. Finished deliverables download from Frame.io and live in your Birdline workspace. RAW footage, photos, and project files are handed over by WeTransfer or on a drive, depending on size.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Every partnership is a rolling monthly retainer with no fixed term: cancel anytime with 60 days’ notice. That window lets us finish open projects and shoots, hand everything to your team, and deliver every asset.
Do I have to use the studio to use the app?
No. The Birdline App works on its own for any team. The Monthly Partnership adds our creative team to it.
Do I have to move my docs out of Google or Notion?
No. Your files stay where they are. Birdline pulls in the live details, then adds what a doc can't hold: deliverables with assignees and status, a line-item budget, and a generated shot list.
Which ad platforms can I connect?
Meta and Google Ads today. TikTok and LinkedIn are on our roadmap.
What are ROPS™ and ROCS™?
ROPS™ (return on production spend) is campaign revenue divided by what the production cost. ROCS™ (return on campaign spend) divides the same revenue by production and ad spend together. The line-item budget in your brief supplies the costs; the ads you connect in Insights supply the revenue.
How do we start?
Book a call. We'll assess the creative content you need, scope the right package, and set up your workspace with your first campaign. Onboarding is simple and fast, so we can get started within a week and begin executing right away. If you only want the app, start the free trial and bring the campaign you're working on now.
