FAQ

Questions About Motion Graphics.

What’s the difference between Motion Graphics and 3D?
Motion graphics is 2D design in motion: type, shapes, illustration and UI. 3D builds objects and environments with depth and light. Many pieces use both, and the same Birds work on each.
Can you animate designs we already have?
Yes. Send the static ads, illustrations or brand assets and we bring them to life, keeping your type, colors and layouts intact.
Do we get the project files?
Yes. You own the media and the files. We deliver the source projects along with the exports, and a motion toolkit your team can use.
