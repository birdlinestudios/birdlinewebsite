What it is

A Show Is a Content Engine.

A video podcast is a content engine when it’s produced like one. We set up the studio or your space, run a multi-camera shoot with broadcast audio, and edit the full episode for YouTube and the audio platforms.

Then the episode becomes a week of content: vertical clips with captions for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, quote cards and audiograms, all tracked as deliverables in the Birdline App so nothing waits on the next recording. WHOOP’s partnership with us grew to include video podcasts alongside paid social, design and VFX.

  • Best forFounder and expert shows, customer conversations, and brand series on YouTube.
  • DeliverablesFull episodes, vertical clips with captions, audio masters, quote cards and thumbnails.
  • How it’s scopedRecording is scoped as a production, per episode or per season. Edits and clips run on the Monthly Partnership.
  • TurnaroundThe episode and its clips within a week of recording on average.

Why it works

The process

From Brief to Delivery.

Every project runs through the Birdline App: the brief, the shot list, every deliverable with its owner and due date, and the results that brief the next one.

  1. 01

    Format & set

    The show’s format, cadence and look, and the set built or booked for it.

  2. 02

    Record

    Multi-camera capture with broadcast audio, run by a Birdline crew.

  3. 03

    Edit the episode

    The full cut for YouTube and the audio master for podcast platforms, with titles and show branding.

  4. 04

    Clip & caption

    Vertical clips, quote cards, audiograms and thumbnails, cut and captioned for each platform.

  5. 05

    Publish & track

    Everything lands in Deliverables with its due date, so the week’s posts are ready before the next recording.

Talk to us on Slack, review on Frame.io, and track it all in the weekly project tracker: the Birdline Workflow, included with every package.

FAQ

Questions About Video Podcasts.

Can you record in our office?
Yes. We bring the cameras, lights and audio to your space, or set the show up in a studio from our network.
How many clips per episode?
As many as the episode holds. On the Monthly Partnership clips are unlimited deliverables, so we cut every usable moment.
Do you handle the show’s branding?
Yes. Titles, lower thirds, cover art and thumbnails come from the same team, on brand with the rest of your content.
