Overview

What We Do for Route

We partnered with Route as their always-on content team, producing a steady flow of video, photography, and animations to power their social channels, website, app, and live events. From profile features and “How-To” tutorials to high-impact B2B commercials, our work spanned a wide range of formats designed to connect with multiple audiences.

Beyond production, we collaborated closely with Route’s creative and marketing teams to align every project with their brand strategy. Together, we built storyboards, crafted shot lists, and developed detailed creative briefs—ensuring each asset was purposeful, on-brand, and optimized for measurable impact.
