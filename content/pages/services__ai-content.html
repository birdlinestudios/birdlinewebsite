What it is

Directed, Not Just Prompted.

Generative tools are part of how we work now, from the shot lists and visuals the Birdline App generates for a brief to concept frames, product renders, background extensions and variations that would take days by hand. The craft is in the direction: references, the brand library, and a creative who knows what the brand looks like.

We use it to support creative projects, not replace them: to show you the shot before the shoot, to render a product that isn’t built yet, to turn one approved visual into fifty variations for testing, and to draft a scratch voice-over before the real session.

  • Best forConcept and pitch visuals, product renders, variations for testing, background extension, and scratch VO.
  • DeliverablesGenerated visuals and audio finished by our designers and editors, in every size, with the sources kept on file.
  • How it’s scopedPart of the work on both packages. Larger generative builds are scoped as a project.
  • TurnaroundConcept frames in days; finished pieces within a week on average.

The process

From Brief to Delivery.

Every project runs through the Birdline App: the brief, the shot list, every deliverable with its owner and due date, and the results that brief the next one.

  1. 01

    Brief & brand library

    The goal, the references, and the logos and products the generations have to stay true to.

  2. 02

    Generate concepts

    Several directions as frames, from the shot list visuals in the app or a dedicated session.

  3. 03

    Direct & refine

    A creative picks, prompts and iterates toward the brief, the way a director works a shot.

  4. 04

    Finish by hand

    Designers and editors retouch, composite and color the selects so they hold up next to your photography.

  5. 05

    Deliver & document

    Every size in Deliverables, with what was generated noted and the sources on file.

FAQ

Questions About AI Content.

Will people know it’s AI?
We tell you what was generated, and anything customer-facing is finished by our designers and editors so it holds up next to your photography. Where a platform or your policy requires disclosure, we label it.
What do you generate with?
A mix of tools chosen per task, always under a creative’s direction. Your brand library in the Birdline App, logos and products, anchors every generation so the visuals stay on brand.
Can AI replace a shoot?
Sometimes it should: a render before the product exists, a variation that doesn’t need a crew. For the hero spot, real people and real light still win, and our Birds know the difference.
