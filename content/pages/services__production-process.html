How a production runs

From the Call to the Library.

Every production runs the same way, whatever the medium.

  1. 01

    Scope it

    On the call we scope the shoot to its creative goals, medium, location and budget.

  2. 02

    Brief & shot list

    A Smart Brief in the Birdline App, with a generated shot list and the props, wardrobe and locations it needs.

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    Casting, locations, crew, schedule and a line-item budget.

  4. 04

    On set

    Our Birds shoot the list, video and stills on the same day, with selects reviewed as the day goes.

  5. 05

    Post & delivery

    Edit, color, sound and retouching, cut for every placement. Initial assets within a week on average.

  6. 06

    Into the library

    Every asset stays tied to its brief, ready for the Monthly Partnership to keep cutting from it.

How we measure productions

Know What Your Content Is Worth.

ROAS tells you how media performed. It says nothing about the shoot behind it. ROPS™ and ROCS™ put production on the same page as ad spend, so every campaign shows what the content actually earned.

  • ROASRevenue ÷ ad spend
    • Ad spend
    • Production

    How efficiently the media converted. Production isn’t in it, so a $100K shoot and a $5K shoot look exactly the same.

  • ROCS™Revenue ÷ production + ad spend
    • Ad spend
    • Production

    What the campaign cost all in, and what it returned. The number that holds up when finance asks why.

  • ROPS™Revenue ÷ production spend
    • Ad spend
    • Production

    What one day on set earned, counted across every ad, cutdown, and campaign that came from it, this quarter and the next.
