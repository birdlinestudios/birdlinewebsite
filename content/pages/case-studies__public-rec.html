Overview

What We Do for Public Rec

We provide Public Rec with a dedicated content team, ensuring a steady flow of high-quality video, photography, and edits each month. Our Birds handle recurring shoots to capture their full product line, leveraging top-tier talent and production crews to create compelling ads, social content, TV commercials, and more.

Beyond production, we collaborate directly with their creative and marketing teams—developing large-scale commercial campaigns, refining performance-driven edits, and continuously optimizing content through real-time feedback loops. This ongoing partnership fuels their direct response ad success across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube.

Results

What It Earned

From 2021 to 2025, Public Rec invested under $1M in Birdline productions and creative partnership, turning that into over $53M in returns across TV and Meta.

For TV alone, $8M in total spend (including production) generated $32M in revenue, a 4x return, with one 30-second spot achieving a 51.7x ROAS—all from existing footage.

As a result of our strategic collaboration, 7 national TV commercials, and compounding creative production process, Public Rec attracted its first institutional investor in 2023.
