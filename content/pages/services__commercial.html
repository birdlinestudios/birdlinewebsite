What it is

The Anchor of the Campaign.

A commercial sets the story, the look and the message that everything else in a campaign borrows from. We produce them as full-service productions: concept and script, casting and locations, a crew matched to the idea, and post-production through final color and mix.

Because every shoot is planned in the Birdline App, the hero spot is never the only deliverable. The same footage feeds cutdowns, verticals, stills and paid variations for months, so one production keeps paying for itself. For Public Rec, one 30-second spot cut from existing footage returned a 51.7× ROAS.

  • Best forLaunches, seasonal campaigns, brand stories, and TV or CTV flights.
  • DeliverablesThe hero spot plus :30, :15 and :06 cutdowns, with vertical and square versions.
  • How it’s scopedA production, scoped per project. Edits and new versions run on the Monthly Partnership.
  • TurnaroundThe shoot is set on the call around your launch; initial cuts within a week of it on average.

FAQ

Questions About Commercial.

Do we need a Monthly Partnership to book a commercial?
No. Productions are scoped on their own, per project. Most partners add one to a Video Editing or Design & Animation package so the footage keeps turning into new deliverables every month, but a single production stands on its own.
Who’s on the crew?
Our Birds: a director, DP, producer and the crew the concept calls for, from a team whose credits include Red Bull, Nike, Nestlé and Marvel. Every production is cast and crewed to its idea, medium and budget.
What do we get back besides the spot?
Every cutdown and version scoped in the brief, the stills from set, and the project files. You own the media, and on the Monthly Partnership we keep cutting from the footage with unlimited edits and revisions.
