Getting started

Get Set Up with Birdline.

We start with a kickoff to meet your team, learn how you work, and decide what to tackle first.

  • Meet your team

    You’ll meet your dedicated project manager and the Birds who’ll be making the work.

  • Share what you have

    Brand guidelines, past campaigns, raw footage, and working files. We’ll organize what comes in and flag what we can start using right away.

  • Get connected

    We connect both teams in Slack and set you up in the Birdline App. The app is included in your Monthly Partnership, and your team can use it for its own projects, too.

How do unlimited deliverables work?

Send as many requests and revisions as you need within the scope of your Monthly Partnership. We agree on what comes first and work through the queue in priority order. A typical first edit takes about a week, depending on complexity and what’s already in the queue.

Productions

Shoot for This Campaign and the Next One.

New productions are scoped and billed separately. We start with what the campaign needs, agree on the scope and budget, and handle the shot list, crew, talent, locations, schedule, and call sheets as needed.

On set, we capture the hero assets alongside alternate hooks, product details, clean takes, and framing we can use in future edits. Over time, that gives you a library you can pull from for a new paid ad, a product launch, or a TV spot. For Gardyn, we used AI and a single photo from an earlier campaign to create the first ten seconds of a new ad.

The library

How We Keep Footage Easy to Find.

We store active footage on high-capacity SSDs for fast access. Consistent folder structures and clear file names make it easy for an editor to pull a shot from two years ago and cut it alongside something we filmed last month.

In the Birdline App, the approved creative stays connected to the campaign and brief it came from.

ROPS™ & ROCS™

See What the Work Returned.

When performance data is connected in Birdline, we can trace ad results back to the creative, the brief, and the production behind it.

  • ROPS™Return on Production Spend

    How much tracked revenue did the creative generate for each dollar spent making it?

  • ROCS™Return on Campaign Spend

    How did the campaign perform when you account for both production and media spend?

We bring those results into the next strategy call. Sometimes the best next move is a new edit from footage you already have. Sometimes the numbers give us a reason to bring a proven setup back for another shoot.
