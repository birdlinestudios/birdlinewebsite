How a partnership runs

From Kickoff to Final.

  1. Your project manager and Birds
    01

    Kickoff

    We meet your team, learn how you work, and decide what to tackle first. You’ll meet your project manager and the Birds who’ll be making the work. Send over brand guidelines, past campaigns, and footage; we’ll organize what comes in.

  2. Hero spot · 30s cutdowns v2Brief · 9 replies
    02

    Slack and the Birdline App

    Both teams in one channel. Each project gets a thread linked to its brief in Birdline, where you talk directly with the people making the work and see where things stand. The app is included in your Monthly Partnership.

  3. BriefDraftFinal
    03

    Brief to final

    Start a brief in the app or drop a request in Slack. Drafts go to Frame.io for timecoded notes, and every deliverable is tracked against its brief. Approved finals land in Birdline, ready to launch and easy to find again.

  4. 7shipped4in progress5up next
    04

    Strategy calls

    Every other week, or weekly when needed: what shipped, what’s in progress, what’s performing, and what to make next, from the library or a new shoot.

Unlimited deliverables Send as many requests and revisions as you need within the scope of your Monthly Partnership. We agree on what comes first and work through the queue in priority order. A typical first edit takes about a week.

Productions and the library

Shoot for This Campaign and the Next One.

On set

New productions are scoped and billed separately. We agree on the scope and budget, then handle the shot list, crew, talent, locations, schedule, and call sheets. On set, we capture the hero assets alongside alternate hooks, product details, and clean takes, so there’s a library to pull from for the next paid ad, product launch, or TV spot. For Gardyn, AI and a single photo from an earlier campaign became the first ten seconds of a new ad.

In the library

Active footage lives on fast SSDs with consistent folder structures and clear file names, so an editor can pull a shot from two years ago and cut it beside something we filmed last month. In the Birdline App, the approved creative stays connected to the campaign and brief it came from.

ROPS™ & ROCS™

See What the Work Returned.

When performance data is connected in Birdline, we trace ad results back to the creative, the brief, and the production behind it.

  • ROPS™Return on Production Spend

    How much tracked revenue did the creative generate for each dollar spent making it?

  • ROCS™Return on Campaign Spend

    How did the campaign perform when you account for both production and media spend?

Those results shape the next strategy call: sometimes a new edit from footage you already have, sometimes a reason to bring a proven setup back for another shoot.
