Frequently Asked Questions.

What counts as a deliverable?
Any video edit, cutdown, design, or animation your team requests through the workspace: paid social ads, organic clips, statics, landing page graphics, motion pieces, and the iterations on all of them. Productions that need a shoot (video, photography, UGC) are scoped separately, per project.
How fast do deliverables come back?
Most edits come back within the week, and many sooner. Bigger pieces get a timeline up front. Every request has an owner, a due date, and a review status in the workspace, so you always know when it lands.
Do I own the media and the project files?
Yes. You own everything we create under the agreement. Finished deliverables download from Frame.io and live in your Birdline workspace. RAW footage, photos, and project files are handed over by WeTransfer or on a drive, depending on size.
What do we cover on recurring strategy calls?
We meet every other week, with weekly calls as needed. We review what’s been delivered, what’s in progress, and what’s coming up. We also discuss which content is performing well and where there’s room to improve, then use what we learn to set the next creative priorities. That could mean creating new deliverables from existing assets or planning a new production.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Every partnership is a rolling monthly retainer with no fixed term: cancel anytime with 60 days’ notice. That window lets us finish open projects and shoots, hand everything to your team, and deliver every asset.
How do we start?
Book a call. We'll assess the creative content you need, scope the right package, and set up your workspace with your first campaign. Onboarding is simple and fast, so we can get started within a week and begin executing right away. If you only want the app, start the free trial and bring the campaign you're working on now.
