What it is

Volume and Iteration, Every Week.

Paid social runs on volume and iteration. We produce the ads and the variations the platforms need, new hooks, new openers, different lengths and ratios, so your media team always has fresh creative to test instead of fatiguing on the same three ads.

On the Monthly Partnership this is unlimited: a dedicated editor and designer turning briefs into ads every week, with ad performance reviews that decide what we make next. Gardyn’s paid content lifted CTR 28% year over year and watch-through 50%; Public Rec turned under $1M of production into $53M+ across TV and Meta.

FAQ

Questions About Paid Social.

How many ads can we request?
As many as you need. Deliverables are unlimited on the Monthly Partnership; your dedicated editor and designer work the queue in priority order and deliver weekly, and revisions are unlimited too.
Do you need new footage?
No. Most paid social starts from the library you already have, past shoots, product photography and UGC, and we add productions when a campaign needs new material.
How do you know what’s working?
Connect Meta in the Birdline App and every ad ties back to its campaign, with revenue, ad spend and production spend side by side. We review it on recurring calls and brief the next round from it.
