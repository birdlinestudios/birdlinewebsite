What it is

Authentic by Design.

UGC-style video borrows the language of the feed: direct to camera, phone-shot texture, real settings, real people. We script the beats that make it convert, cast creators or real customers, and direct it so it stays authentic while still landing the message.

It pairs with everything else we make. A brand commercial sets the story, UGC proves it in a customer’s voice, and paid social puts both to work. For Patrol we scripted, shot and edited a UGC campaign with real families in their own homes, and the short-form assets pulled from it ran across Instagram and Meta.

  • Best forPaid social, product demos, testimonials, unboxings, and comment-response videos.
  • Deliverables9:16 videos in several hooks and lengths, captioned, with sound-on and sound-off versions.
  • How it’s scopedCasting and shoots are scoped as a production. Edits and versions are unlimited on the Monthly Partnership.
  • TurnaroundInitial assets within a week on average once the footage is in.

The process

From Brief to Delivery.

Every project runs through the Birdline App: the brief, the shot list, every deliverable with its owner and due date, and the results that brief the next one.

  1. 01

    Brief & angle

    The claim the video has to prove, the audience, and the hooks worth testing.

  2. 02

    Script & casting

    Beats for each piece and creators or customers cast to the brief, with releases handled.

  3. 03

    Capture

    Creators film to the script in their own space, or our crew shoots it when control matters.

  4. 04

    Edit & caption

    Cut to the hook, captioned, versioned by length and ratio, with the sound-off opener checked.

  5. 05

    Launch & learn

    The set goes live, performance comes back in Insights, and the next session is briefed from it.

FAQ

Questions About UGC.

Do you find the creators?
Yes. We cast creators or real customers to the brief, handle direction and releases, and shoot it ourselves when a crew gives better control over the result.
Will it look too polished?
Only if the brief wants it to. UGC-style means phone texture, natural light and real settings, with the hooks and pacing that make it perform.
How is this different from Paid Social?
UGC is one of the formats we make for paid social. Most feeds mix UGC-style pieces with polished product ads and statics, and we produce all three from the same brief.
