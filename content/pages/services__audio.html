What it is

Half of Every Video, All of Radio.

Sound is half of every video and all of radio and podcasts. Our audio work covers voice-over casting and recording, sound design, music licensing and the final mix, delivered broadcast-legal for TV and radio and platform-ready for podcasts and social.

On the Video Editing package, sound design, the mix and music licensing are included with every edit. Radio spots, streaming audio ads and podcast production are scoped on their own.

FAQ

Questions About Audio.

Is music licensing included?
Yes. Music licensing is part of both packages, and we license tracks for each piece’s placement and term.
Can you produce our podcast without video?
Yes. Audio-only editing, mixing and mastering are scoped per show, with audiograms and quote cards for social if you want them.
Do you record voice-over remotely?
Both. Remote sessions with our VO network, and studio recordings when the spot calls for it.
