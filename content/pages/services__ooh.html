What it is

Where a Campaign Shows Up in the World.

Out-of-home is where a campaign meets people on the street. We design for the surfaces: static billboards and transit shelters, digital screens that loop, booth walls and stadium boards, with type that reads at distance and motion built for silent playback. Public Rec’s mural and Route’s transit shelter are two of ours.

Event and trade-show work rolls in too: the booth’s screens and panels, the recap video and interviews shot on the floor, and the social clips that run while the event is on, as we did for FreePower at KBIS and HD Expo.

  • Best forBillboards and transit, digital OOH loops, trade shows and events, retail and stadium screens, office displays.
  • DeliverablesPrint-ready artwork, digital screen loops in every spec, booth graphics, event recaps and social clips.
  • How it’s scopedArtwork and loops run on the Design & Animation package. Event coverage is scoped as a production.
  • TurnaroundArtwork and loops within a week on average; events are planned to the date.

Why it works

What OOH Does for a Brand.

  • Readable at any distance

    Hierarchy, type size and contrast set for the viewing distance of each placement, from a shelter to a stadium board.

  • Motion for silent screens

    Digital OOH loops designed to land without sound, in the exact pixel specs and durations of each network.

  • From the booth to the feed

    A crew on the floor shoots the booth, the demos and the interviews, so the event keeps working online after it closes.

FAQ

Questions About OOH.

Do you handle the media buy?
No. We design and deliver to the vendor’s specs; placement stays with your media team or the venue.
Can you cover our event?
Yes. A crew shoots the booth, the floor and the interviews, and clips go out while the event is still on.
Do you print?
We deliver print-ready files to your printer or the vendor, checked against their specs, and can manage the handoff.
