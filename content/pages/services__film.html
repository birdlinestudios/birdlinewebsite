What it is

A Look That Can’t Be Faked.

Film still does something digital can’t: the color, the grain, the way highlights roll off. We shoot Super 8 and 16mm for energy and nostalgia, 35mm for the hero spot, and finish each with a scan and grade that keeps the character while meeting modern delivery specs.

It’s a choice we make with you on the brief, when the idea and the brand call for it, and it’s planned so the film day also yields stills and digital coverage for everything around the spot.

  • Best forBrand films, fashion and lifestyle campaigns, music-led spots, and heritage or anniversary stories.
  • DeliverablesA graded film master plus digital cutdowns, verticals and scanned stills.
  • How it’s scopedA production, scoped per project, with stock, processing and scanning in the line-item budget.
  • TurnaroundSet per project. Processing and scanning add days to the schedule, not weeks.

FAQ

Questions About Film.

Is film more expensive?
Stock, processing and scanning add line items, and they show in the brief’s budget. For the right idea the look is worth it, and a hybrid shoot keeps digital coverage for everything else.
Can we mix film and digital?
Yes. Most film productions pair a film camera for the hero moments with digital coverage for social and stills.
What does it deliver as?
The same as any production: a graded master and the cutdowns, verticals and stills your channels need, in your Birdline workspace.
