The process

From Brief to Delivery.

Every project runs through the Birdline App: the brief, the shot list, every deliverable with its owner and due date, and the results that brief the next one.

  1. 01

    Brief & shot list

    The products, the looks and the placements, with the shot list generated in the app and props, wardrobe and locations gathered from it.

  2. 02

    Pre-production

    Studio or location, styling, talent and the schedule, planned with the video crew when the day is shared.

  3. 03

    The shoot

    Our photographer and stylist work the list, reviewing selects with you as the day goes.

  4. 04

    Retouching & crops

    Color, retouching and every crop the brief calls for, with unlimited revisions on the partnership.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    The library lands in Deliverables and your storage, ready for design, ads and the next campaign.

FAQ

Questions About Photography.

Can we combine photo and video in one shoot?
Yes, and we recommend it. Most Birdline productions capture both, with the shot list planned so the stills team works alongside the video crew and nothing on the list is missed.
Who retouches?
Our Birds. Retouching, color and cropping are part of the production, and on the Monthly Partnership further crops, layouts and edits are unlimited.
Do we own the images?
Yes. You own the media and the files. They live in your Birdline workspace and your storage, with no limit on how you use them.
