What it is

Broadcast Reach, Digital Targeting.

Connected TV puts a commercial in front of a household at the scale of broadcast with the targeting of digital. We produce for it end to end: hero spots shot for the big screen, cut to :30 and :15, mastered to each platform’s delivery spec, with closed captions and the audio levels broadcast requires.

It doesn’t always take a new shoot. For Public Rec, one 30-second spot cut from existing footage returned a 51.7× ROAS, and seven national TV commercials over the partnership earned a 4× return on $8M of TV spend, production included.

  • Best forLaunches, seasonal flights, and brands moving from direct response into TV.
  • Deliverables:30 and :15 spots mastered to CTV and OTT specs, with captions, slates and broadcast-legal audio.
  • How it’s scopedNew spots are scoped as a production. Cuts from existing footage run on the Monthly Partnership.
  • TurnaroundCuts from existing footage within a week on average; new productions are set on the call.

FAQ

Questions About CTV.

What’s the difference between CTV and a commercial?
The commercial is the creative; CTV is where it runs. CTV and OTT platforms have their own specs for length, resolution, captions and audio, and we master and deliver to them.
Can you cut a TV spot from footage we already have?
Yes. That’s how Public Rec’s 51.7× spot was made. We review the library, cut, master and deliver.
Do you handle the trafficking?
We deliver files to the specs of your buying platform or agency. Placement and buying stay with your media team.
