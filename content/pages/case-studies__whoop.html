What We Do for WHOOP

Starting in early 2026, our partnership with WHOOP has grown from supporting a social series and a handful of paid ads into a much broader creative partnership spanning Ferrari content, video podcasts, paid social, design, animation, VFX, and more. Birdline has become a flexible extension of WHOOP’s in-house team, scaling alongside their needs and supporting everything from quick-turn content to larger, high-profile productions.
