Overview

What We Do for Gardyn

Our dedicated Bird team powers Gardyn’s content production, delivering high-quality video, photography, and graphics every month for their social media, website, and paid ads. We create a diverse mix of content, including commercial videos, UGC-style assets, still photography, and custom graphic designs tailored to their marketing needs.

We collaborate directly with Gardyn’s creative and marketing teams—developing storyboards, shot lists, creative briefs, and performance-driven strategies to ensure every campaign is optimized for engagement and ROI.

Results

What It Earned

In 2024, Gardyn partnered with Birdline to introduce their new indoor gardening systems and strengthen brand awareness through highly engaging, premium content.

Over the past 12 months, our paid content drove a 28% YoY boost in CTR, while improving watch-through rates by 50%. With $8.23M ad spend, we scaled campaigns by 14%, maintaining cost efficiency with a 9% improvement in CAC.

Through our social assets, Gardyn achieved 45M+ organic views and a 111% follower growth rate, amplifying brand visibility and engagement.
