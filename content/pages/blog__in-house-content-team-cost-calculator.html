Why Wouldn’t I Just Hire a Full-Time Team?

You could. Here is the math.

November 15, 2026·2 min read

Sometimes you should. If the work is one skill, forty hours a week, for years, hire the person.

But ads are not one skill. Shipping them every week takes a team.

  • A senior video editor.
  • A senior designer who animates.
  • A producer who runs the work and the creative strategy.

Senior, because that is who you get with us.

Take a brand shipping 40 ads a month, half video and half design. That takes one of each.

On payroll

  • Three salaries. $306,170 a year, before anything else.
  • The rest. Benefits, payroll taxes, software, tools and drives bring it to $394,133.
  • The search. 39 days to fill each seat.

With Birdline

  • The same team. A dedicated senior editor, a dedicated senior designer/animator and a project manager who is also your creative strategist.
  • $168,000 a year. Both packages, from $14,000 a month. That is $226,133 less.
  • No payroll, no search. The Birdline App is included, and work starts within a week.

Run your numbers

The calculator opens on that brand, with four productions a year. Productions cost the same either way. Every number is yours to change.

When demand moves

Need more? We add a dedicated editor or designer/animator. Need less? Scale back. Every partnership is rolling, with 60 days’ notice to cancel.

A salary stays whether the volume does or not.

A content team that works like an employee and scales like an agency.

When a hire wins

If the calculator says a hire wins, hire. A mid-level editor costs about what our Video Editing package does. A junior editor costs less. The gap opens at senior level: about $40,000 a year on one seat, and more with every skill you add.

And keep the people who own your brand. We are not there to replace them. We are the production capacity around them.

How we got the numbers Salaries, costs, staffing rules and sources
  • Salaries. Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2025, U.S. Senior pay, the 75th percentile, for the editor ($105,310) and the designer/animator ($110,500, the midpoint of graphic designers and animators). The median for the producer ($90,360).
  • Benefits and payroll taxes. 25% of salary, from BLS employer costs for June 2026.
  • Software and equipment. $2,500 a person a year: Adobe Creative Cloud, a workstation and stock.
  • Workflow tools. $300 a person a year for a project tracker and a review tool, at Asana and Frame.io list prices.
  • Drives. Two 4TB editing SSDs for each editor a year, $1,900, and one 8TB backup drive for each production, $280. B&H prices.
  • Hiring. 39 days to fill a seat: SHRM’s 2026 median for nonexecutive roles.
  • Output. On average, one editor ships 20 video ads a month and one designer ships 50 statics or motion pieces. With unlimited deliverables, that moves with your edit requests.
  • The producer. One joins at two makers in a company past ten people, three makers anywhere, or six productions a year. Then one for every eight makers, and one more at a production a month.
  • Creative strategy. At Birdline, your project manager is also your creative strategist for creative marketing. It is always included, so payroll counts one hire for both jobs.
  • Birdline. Starting prices: Video Editing at $8,000 a month and Design & Animation at $6,000 a month, one package for each dedicated creative. The SSDs we edit from are ours.
  • Productions. The same budget on both sides: $20,000 each unless you change it.
  • One editor. At the median salary, $75,420, a single seat costs within $3,000 a year of the Video Editing package.
  • Left out. Recruiting, bonuses, raises, turnover, management time and office space. Each one adds to the payroll side.
