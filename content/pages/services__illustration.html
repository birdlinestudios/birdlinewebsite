What it is

A World of the Brand’s Own.

Illustration gives a brand a world of its own. For Literati’s Everglow Forest Book Fair, a dragon reading to a unicorn became the key art for a whole program, with social posts, print and animation built from it. Illustration works where photography can’t: abstract ideas, characters, maps, and the playful or editorial tone a campaign calls for.

We develop the style with you, references, sketches and a color system, then produce the set, with every piece built in layers so it can be animated, cropped or extended later.

  • Best forCampaign key art, characters and mascots, editorial and explainer visuals, packaging, and social.
  • DeliverablesIllustrations as layered source files and exports in every size, with style frames for future pieces.
  • How it’s scopedIncluded in the Design & Animation package, unlimited. Large illustrated systems are scoped as a project.
  • TurnaroundStyle exploration first, then initial pieces within a week on average.

FAQ

Questions About Illustrations.

Can you match an illustration style we already use?
Yes. We study your existing work and extend it, or develop a new style with you from references.
Can the illustrations be animated?
Yes. We build in layers so Motion Graphics can bring them to life without redrawing, from a looping social post to a full explainer.
Who owns the artwork?
You do. You own the media and the files, and the layered sources are delivered with the exports.
