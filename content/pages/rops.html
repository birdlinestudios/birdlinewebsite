FAQ

Questions About ROPS™ and ROCS™.

Is ROPS™ just another name for ROAS?
No. ROAS divides revenue by ad spend and leaves production out entirely. ROPS™ divides revenue by what the production cost, and ROCS™ by production and ad spend together. The three answer different questions, and you need all of them to know what a campaign was worth.
What counts as tracked revenue?
The revenue attributed to the ads you connect to the campaign in the Birdline App. Connect Meta today, with TikTok and YouTube coming soon, and assign each ad to its campaign; the revenue and ad spend flow in from there.
Where does the production cost come from?
From the line-item budget in the brief: pre-production, production, and post, totaled per brief. Enter it once when the shoot is planned and it carries through to Insights.
Do we need the Monthly Partnership to measure it?
No. The Birdline App measures ROPS™ and ROCS™ on its own, for any team. Start a free 30-day trial and bring the campaign you’re working on now. The Monthly Partnership adds our creative team to the same workspace.
What if a campaign pulls from several shoots?
That’s the point of the library. Each brief carries its own budget, so a campaign cut from three productions shows what it earned against what those productions cost, and each production keeps earning across every campaign that uses it.
