Results

What It Earned

In 2024, FreePower teamed up with Birdline to launch its groundbreaking wireless charging technology and build brand awareness through expertly crafted, engaging content.

Over the past 12 months, Birdline’s efforts delivered 15M total views, 170K link clicks, and a reach of 23M users, while growing FreePower’s audience by 25K+ followers.

By leveraging creative production strategies, Birdline showcased FreePower’s technology without the need for in-home installations, lowering production costs and driving maximizing their content.
