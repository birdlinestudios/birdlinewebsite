What it is

The Volume Play of a Content Pipeline.

Every campaign needs statics in a dozen sizes, an email, a landing page and the brand assets around them, and they all have to arrive on time and on brand. A dedicated designer on the Design & Animation package delivers them weekly, with unlimited revisions.

Because the designer works inside your campaign in the Birdline App, with your brand library at hand, the work stays consistent across channels and compounds over time: a static becomes a series, a landing page becomes a template. Route, Literati and Gardyn each run design this way every month.

FAQ

Questions About Design.

Which tools do you design in?
Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud, and whatever your team already uses. Briefs can sync from Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Notion in the Birdline App.
Can you work inside our existing brand system?
Yes, that’s the default. We work from your brand library and guidelines, and extend the system when a campaign needs something new.
What counts as a deliverable?
A finished piece or its set: one static in all of its sizes, one email, one landing page. Deliverables and revisions are unlimited on the package.
