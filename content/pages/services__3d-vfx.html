What it is

Shoot What Doesn’t Exist Yet.

Some shots are impossible, impractical or too expensive to capture. 3D and VFX fill that gap: photoreal product renders before a product exists, environments built around the brand, and compositing that adds what the camera missed. For FreePower, custom animation and VFX are part of every month’s work, and showing the technology without in-home installations kept production costs down.

We use it where it earns its place, a hero render for a launch, a feature callout on a jacket, a screen replacement in a spot, and keep the look consistent with the photography and video around it. VFX and text animations are part of every edit on the Video Editing package.

  • Best forLaunches before samples exist, tech and feature explainers, and shots that can’t be captured.
  • DeliverablesStill renders, animated product spins and explainers, and VFX shots composited into live action.
  • How it’s scopedVFX and text animations are included with every edit; larger 3D builds and render series are scoped per project.
  • TurnaroundSet per project. A render series usually moves faster than a shoot, with unlimited revisions.

Why it works

What 3D and VFX Do for a Brand.

  • Shoot what doesn’t exist yet

    From CAD files, reference photos or samples we build a model that stands in for the product in renders and animation, before the first unit ships.

  • Consistent with the real thing

    Lighting, lens and color are matched to your photography, so renders sit next to photographs without a seam.

  • Reuse forever

    A built model yields endless angles, colorways and variants; a composited world can host the next campaign too.

FAQ

Questions About 3D / VFX.

Do you need the physical product?
Not always. From CAD files, reference photos or samples we build a model that stands in for the product in renders and animation.
Is VFX included in the Video Editing package?
VFX and text animations are included with every edit. Larger 3D builds and render series are scoped as a production, per project.
Can renders match our photography?
Yes. We match lighting, lens and color to your existing library, so renders and photographs sit side by side on a product page without a seam.
