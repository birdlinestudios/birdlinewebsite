A Full Season of Content, Delivered at Game Speed.

Birdline became the United Football League’s content partner for a new season: a broadcast TV spot, Media Day, a weekly social slate, stadium screens and game-day radio for all 8 teams.

  • ClientUFL
  • ServicesProduction, Broadcast TV Spot, Social Video, In-Stadium Video, Radio
Overview

What We Do for UFL

The United Football League was launching a new season across 8 teams. They needed fresh, high-quality content for every team, every week. It had to work on social feeds, on stadium screens, on the radio and on broadcast TV, and it had to arrive in time for game day.

Birdline became the UFL’s content partner from start to finish.

The challenge

What UFL Needed

  • Fresh, high-quality content for every team, every week, across a new 8-team season.
  • Work that fits social feeds, stadium screens, radio and broadcast TV.
  • Every piece in time for game day.
The solution

How We Built It.

  1. 01

    A broadcast TV spot with NOBULL

    In under a month, we produced “What No Can Do,” a broadcast TV spot for the UFL × NOBULL partnership. It features Jordan Ta’amu and Hakeem Butler.

  2. 02

    Media Day production

    Our crew captured player interviews and content for the league’s teams in one high-energy shoot at the Arlington, TX stadium.

  3. 03

    A weekly content engine

    We turned that footage into a 10-week slate of social videos. Each week was planned around the league’s game schedule.

  4. 04

    Made for the stadium

    We produced widescreen edits for in-stadium screens. Fans saw the players on the big screen on game day.

  5. 05

    Radio that moves fast

    We created game-day radio spots for teams across the league. Many were turned around in a day, including playoff promos with last-minute changes.

  6. 06

    A true partnership

    The UFL kept bringing us more work as the season went on. The radio program grew well beyond its original scope.

Results

What It Earned

A season of content across four channels, delivered on schedule for all 8 teams, and a relationship that carried on after the final game.
