Overview

What We Do for Patrol

We partnered with Patrol as a full-service content team, leading creative development and production across campaigns that build awareness, trust, and adoption for the Patrol app. From premium brand commercials to authentic UGC storytelling, we’ve helped shape Patrol’s message around proactive, community-funded security — showing real families taking protection into their own hands.

What makes our partnership effective is the integration of our strategic creative approach with a nimble, always-on production workflow. We work end-to-end — from scripting through final edit — so the Patrol team can focus on growth while we handle the storytelling. Each piece of content is designed to drive performance now and feed the next campaign, creating a snowball effect of momentum and message consistency. We operate as an extension of the Patrol team — not just a vendor, but a production partner.

Results

What It Earned

  1. Brand Commercials

    We launched two high-quality, narrative-driven brand spots titled “When Seconds Matter” and “Private Patrol” that position Patrol as a game-changing tool for community safety.

  2. UGC Campaigns

    We scripted, shot, and edited multiple user-generated-style videos. Our UGC pieces were designed to feel authentic and relatable, featuring real families, natural settings, and direct-to-camera storytelling.

  3. Social Content & Paid Ads

    We created numerous short-form assets for Instagram and Meta, pulling from both our brand commercials and UGC campaigns. Our Private Patrol spot on X alone generated 12M views, 4K reposts, 8K likes, 3K saves, and 1.5K comments. All in one week.
