What it is

The Story Behind the Product.

A profile tells the story behind the product: the founder, the rider, the customer whose life it changed. Shot documentary-style with interviews and real environments, it’s the content people watch to the end, and the source of a season of shorter pieces. Route’s profile features, 686’s rider and designer stories, and Solidroad’s founder and customer interviews all started this way.

We produce the full arc: pre-interviews and story outlines, a small crew that puts subjects at ease, and an edit that finds the narrative. Then we cut it down, trailers, vertical clips and quotes, so one profile fills a month of social.

  • Best forFounder and team stories, customer features, rider and athlete profiles, and YouTube series.
  • DeliverablesA long-form film or episode, a trailer, vertical clips, and pull-quote stills.
  • How it’s scopedA production, scoped per project or per series. Cutdowns and clips run on the Monthly Partnership.
  • TurnaroundSet per project; clips and the trailer within a week of the main cut on average.

FAQ

Questions About Profiles.

How long is a profile?
Whatever the story needs. Most run a few minutes for web and YouTube, with a 60-second trailer and vertical clips alongside.
Can this become a series?
Yes, and series are where profiles earn the most. A repeatable format, a recurring crew and a release cadence turn one shoot into a channel.
Do you handle the interviews?
Yes. Our directors run the pre-interviews and the on-camera conversation, so subjects know the arc before the camera rolls.
